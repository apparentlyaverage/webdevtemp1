Our Story

Located right here in Makhanda, Nic's Nest is more than just a cafe—it is a second home. Set inside a beautifully converted residential house, we offer a cozy retreat complete with original wooden floors and a blazing fireplace for those chilly Eastern Cape mornings.

Whether you are stopping by for our famous early bird breakfast, grabbing a fresh sourdough loaf, or settling in with a warm cappuccino, our friendly staff is here to make your day a little bit brighter.

Our Community

We pride ourselves on being a true gathering place. From long-time locals catching up over a morning brew to Rhodes University students powering through study sessions between lectures, our doors are open to everyone.

Our commitment to quality means we pour care into every cup and every fresh loaf of sourdough. We believe great food and great company go hand-in-hand. Come pull up a chair.
